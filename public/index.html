    Why you can’t paste a real SOC 2 report

    SOC 2 reports are confidential. Vendors share them under NDA, usually through a trust portal, so please don’t paste one here. Use something public instead:

    • A SOC 3 report. It’s the public summary of a SOC 2, and many SaaS and cloud companies post theirs on their trust or security page.
    • An ISO 27001 certificate. Often published with its scope and expiry date.
    • A trust or security page. Paste the text. It’s a good test of the AI checks, since many describe how customer data is used for training.

    Fill in the intake for how your company would use the vendor. The tier, and so the verdict, depends on it.
